What it covers

A defined benchmark, not a vibe.

The standard evaluates the whole team the way honest scrutiny would — because certification is a benchmark, not the goal.

  • Scent-detection reliability under realistic, field-representative conditions.
  • Independent work — blind and double-blind testing to prove the dog reads odor, not the handler.
  • Handler communication, control, and the ability to explain the dog's behavior.
  • Documented, repeatable evaluation — including honest records of failure.
